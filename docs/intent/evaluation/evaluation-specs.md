# Evaluation — EARS Specs

Facets: `IN` (inputs read from the archive), `MATCH` (matchups of surface and monitor), `METRIC`
(metric definitions), `SPLIT` (subsets and splits), `AVAIL` (availability report), `CALSE`
(calibration fit covariance), `TOTVAR` (analytic total variance), `MC` (Monte Carlo propagation),
`DECOMP` (residual decomposition), `SENS` (variogram sensitivity), `BUDGET` (error budget), `FIG`
(report figures), `RUN` (entry point and report), `CFG` (settings and tests), `LOMO`
(leave-one-monitor-out), `BASE` (baselines), `COND` (conditional bias), `UNC` (coverage diagnostics),
`SPIKE` (spike points), `POOL` (pooling within the hour), `MON` (per monitor), `VARIO` (variogram
classes), `LAG` (timing alignment), `FLAG` (flag rates), `ANOM` (common offset and within-hour
anomaly).

## Inputs

- [x] **EVAL-IN-001**: When evaluating a window, evaluation shall read every input from the archive through the observation store's `read_partitioned`, `read_observations`, `read_sites`, and `partition_objects`, and the surface rasters by their `surface_uri`, and shall not read from PostGIS.
- [x] **EVAL-IN-002**: Evaluation shall write nothing to the archive.
- [x] **EVAL-IN-003**: Evaluation shall require the window's `start` and `end` to be timezone-aware, convert each to UTC and truncate it to the hour, evaluate the hours `H` with `start <= H < end`, and fail with a validation error if either is naive or `end <= start` after truncation.
- [x] **EVAL-IN-004**: Evaluation shall read the AirNow observations over `[start − 1 h, end + 1 h)` and form matchups only from those at hours inside the window.

## Matchups

- [x] **EVAL-MATCH-001**: For every hour `H` in the window whose `surface_fits` row has `status = kriged`, evaluation shall form one matchup for each AirNow observation at `H` with non-null `pm25_raw` whose monitor lies inside the grid and whose raster cell is not NaN.
- [x] **EVAL-MATCH-002**: When forming a matchup, evaluation shall set `surface` and `variance` to bands 1 and 2 of the raster cell containing the monitor's EPSG:26918 coordinates, found by inverting the raster's geotransform, with column `i` covering `[x_min + i·res, x_min + (i+1)·res)` and row `j` covering `(y_max − (j+1)·res, y_max − j·res]`.
- [x] **EVAL-MATCH-003**: When forming a matchup, evaluation shall set `obs` to the monitor's `pm25_raw`, `qc_flags` to the observation's flags, `flagged` to whether `qc_flags` is non-empty, `sd = √variance`, `error = surface − obs`, and `z = error / sd`, with `z` null when `sd = 0`.
- [x] **EVAL-MATCH-004**: When forming the matchups of hour `H`, evaluation shall rebuild the hour's kriging-input sensors from the archive with fusion's `hour_sensors` and `hour_points`, set `n_sensors` to the number of sensor rows, `pooled_share` and `fitted_share` to the fraction of those rows with `fit_status` `pooled` and `fitted`, and `nearest_sensor_km` to the distance in kilometres from the monitor to the nearest kriging point.
- [x] **EVAL-MATCH-005**: When the rebuilt kriging inputs of hour `H` have an `n_sensors` or `n_points` different from the hour's `surface_fits` row, evaluation shall set `inputs_reproduced` false on the hour's matchups and keep them in every accuracy metric.
- [x] **EVAL-MATCH-006**: When reading a kriged hour's raster, evaluation shall compare the SHA-256 of its bytes with the row's `surface_sha256` and, on a mismatch, still score the raster and record the hour as mismatched in the availability report.
- [x] **EVAL-MATCH-007**: When forming the matchups of hour `H`, evaluation shall set `points_mean` to the mean of the hour's kriging-point values and `pooled_share_10km` to the fraction with `fit_status` `pooled` among the hour's kriging-input sensors within 10 km of the monitor, null when none is within 10 km.

## Metrics

- [x] **EVAL-METRIC-001**: For a set of matchups, evaluation shall compute `n`, `bias` (mean `error`), `mae` (mean `|error|`), `rmse` (√ mean `error²`), `r2` (`1 − Σ error² / Σ (obs − mean obs)²`), `pearson_r` (Pearson correlation of `surface` and `obs`), and `pearson_r2` (`pearson_r²`), with `r2`, `pearson_r`, and `pearson_r2` null when `n < 2` or either variance is zero.
- [x] **EVAL-METRIC-002**: For a set of matchups, evaluation shall compute `cov1_krig` and `cov2_krig` as the fraction of matchups with non-null `z` having `|z| <= 1` and `|z| <= 2`, `cov1_total` and `cov2_total` as the fraction of matchups with non-null `z_total` and `inputs_reproduced` having `|z_total| <= 1` and `|z_total| <= 2`, and `cov1_total_raw` and `cov2_total_raw` likewise from `z_total_raw`, each null when no matchup qualifies.
- [x] **EVAL-METRIC-003**: For each metric of a metric-table or baseline-table row other than `n`, evaluation shall report `<metric>_ci_low` and `<metric>_ci_high` as the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles of the metric over 1000 block-bootstrap resamples, each drawing the row's UTC days with replacement as many times as the row has days and recomputing the metric over the drawn days' matchups, with the generator seeded with `seed`; both shall be null when the row's matchups span fewer than two days.
- [x] **EVAL-METRIC-004**: Evaluation shall compute the day-bootstrap intervals of every other statistic it reports with an interval, except those of leave one monitor out, by the resampling of EVAL-METRIC-003.

## Subsets and Splits

- [x] **EVAL-SPLIT-001**: Evaluation shall compute every metric for two subsets — `unflagged` (matchups with `flagged` false) and `all` — and shall report `unflagged` as the headline.
- [x] **EVAL-SPLIT-002**: Within each subset, evaluation shall compute the metrics overall and per group of each split: observed level `level` (`<5` for obs < 5, `5–9` for 5 ≤ obs ≤ 9, `>9` for obs > 9 µg/m³), predicted level `pred_level`, `hour_mean_points`, `hour_mean_obs`, UTC hour of day, UTC calendar day, pooled-share band, `spike_hour`, `spike_day`, `variogram`, and `monitor` (the matchup's `site_id`).
- [x] **EVAL-SPLIT-003**: When forming a tertile split, evaluation shall cut the split's values at their tertiles, merge coinciding edges, and label each band with its edges — over the hourly `pooled_share` of the window's kriged hours for pooled-share bands, the subset's matchup `surface` values for `pred_level`, the hourly `points_mean` of the window's kriged hours for `hour_mean_points`, and the hourly mean monitor `obs` of the window's kriged hours with a matchup for `hour_mean_obs`.
- [x] **EVAL-SPLIT-004**: Evaluation shall write the metrics as one table with columns `subset`, `split`, `group`, followed by the metrics of EVAL-METRIC-001 and EVAL-METRIC-002 with `pearson_r2` immediately after `r2`, and the intervals of EVAL-METRIC-003.
- [x] **EVAL-SPLIT-005**: `summary.md` shall label the `hour_mean_obs` split *observed-conditioned*, label `r2` "R² (skill, 1 − SSE/SS_obs)" and `pearson_r2` "r² (squared correlation)" wherever both appear, and present the `pred_level` split beside the `level` split.

## Availability

- [x] **EVAL-AVAIL-001**: Evaluation shall report the number of kriged hours, the number of distinct UTC days with at least one kriged hour, every hour in the window with no `surface_fits` row, and every hour whose row is not `kriged` with its status.
- [x] **EVAL-AVAIL-002**: Evaluation shall report, per AirNow monitor, whether it lies inside the grid, its observations in kriged hours, its matchups, its observations with null `pm25_raw`, its observations at NaN cells, its flagged observations, and the number of the window's kriging-input sensors whose `fitted` fit references it as `ref_site_id`, with their median `distance_m`.
- [x] **EVAL-AVAIL-003**: Evaluation shall report the hours with `inputs_reproduced` false and the hours whose raster does not match its `surface_sha256`.
- [x] **EVAL-AVAIL-004**: When the window has no kriged hour, evaluation shall complete, write the report with empty metric tables, skip the Monte Carlo, leave one monitor out, the baselines, the spike re-krige, the pooling regression, the conditional-bias and coverage diagnostics, the maps, and every figure drawn from matchups, and state in the summary that no hour was kriged.

## Conditional Bias

- [x] **EVAL-COND-001**: Evaluation shall bin the unflagged matchups into deciles of `surface` and write, per bin, its count, mean `surface`, mean `obs`, and the day-bootstrap 95% interval of the mean `obs`, as `reliability.csv`.
- [x] **EVAL-COND-002**: Evaluation shall compute, over the unflagged matchups, the OLS slope of `obs` on `surface` and the OLS slope of `surface` on `obs`, each with its day-bootstrap 95% interval, and write them to `conditional_bias.csv`.
- [x] **EVAL-COND-003**: `summary.md` shall call the surface over-smoothed when the interval of the slope of `obs` on `surface` lies wholly above 1, over-dispersed when it lies wholly below 1, and not distinguishable from calibrated otherwise; and shall state that the surface reads high in clean air and low in dirty air only when the bias interval of the lowest `pred_level` band of the `unflagged` subset lies wholly above 0 and that of the highest band wholly below 0.

## Calibration Uncertainty

- [x] **EVAL-CALSE-001**: For each `(site_id, fit_as_of)` with `fit_status = fitted` and each `fit_as_of` with `fit_status = pooled` used by a kriging-input sensor in the window, evaluation shall rebuild the fit's pairs as `fit_calibrations` does — PurpleAir and AirNow observations over `[fit_as_of − window_days, fit_as_of)` from `read_observations`, aggregated with calibration's `aggregate_hourly`, passed to `calibration_pairs` — using the calibration settings from the environment, taking the sensor's own pairs for `fitted` and the pooled pairs for `pooled`.
- [x] **EVAL-CALSE-002**: For rebuilt pairs with `n >= 3`, evaluation shall compute `σ̂² = Σ residual² / (n − 2)` about the OLS line through the pairs, `Var(b) = σ̂² / Sxx`, `Var(a) = σ̂² (1/n + x̄² / Sxx)`, and `Cov(a, b) = −x̄ σ̂² / Sxx`.
- [x] **EVAL-CALSE-003**: Evaluation shall inflate `Var(a)`, `Var(b)`, and `Cov(a, b)` by `n / n_eff`, with `n_eff = n (1 − ρ₁) / (1 + ρ₁)` and `ρ₁` the lag-1 autocorrelation of the residuals over pairs of consecutive hours within one sensor's time-ordered series, clipped to `[0, 0.99]`, and shall report the uncorrected values beside the corrected ones.
- [x] **EVAL-CALSE-004**: When rebuilt pairs number fewer than three, evaluation shall record no covariance for the fit, and the matchups of every hour in which a kriging-input sensor uses that fit shall carry a null `cal_variance`.
- [x] **EVAL-CALSE-005**: Evaluation shall write a fit-uncertainty table with, per fit, the status, `n`, `ρ₁`, `n_eff`, `σ̂²`, the corrected and uncorrected `Var(a)`, `Var(b)`, `Cov(a, b)`, and the stored and rebuilt `n_pairs`, `slope`, and `intercept`, and shall not fail when stored and rebuilt values differ.

## Total Variance

- [x] **EVAL-TOTVAR-001**: When computing a matchup's calibration term, evaluation shall solve the ordinary-kriging system for the weights `λ` at the monitor's coordinates from the hour's kriging points and the hour's fixed variogram — the row's `variogram_model` with PyKrige parameters `[sill − nugget, range_m, nugget]` — using PyKrige's variogram functions, such that `λ` sums to 1 and `λᵀ z` equals the unclamped estimate at the same location of a PyKrige `OrdinaryKriging` built on the same points with those parameters fixed to within a relative tolerance of 1e-6.
- [x] **EVAL-TOTVAR-002**: Evaluation shall build the sensor covariance `Σ_s` of an hour's kriging-input sensors with diagonal `[1, x_i] C_i [1, x_i]ᵀ + σ̂²_i`, off-diagonal `[1, x_i] C_p [1, x_j]ᵀ` for two `pooled` sensors using the same `fit_as_of`, and 0 otherwise, where `x_i` is the sensor's `pm25_corrected_mean`, `C_i` its fit's corrected covariance matrix of `(a, b)`, and `σ̂²_i` its fit's residual variance.
- [x] **EVAL-TOTVAR-003**: Evaluation shall set `cal_variance = λᵀ M Σ_s Mᵀ λ`, where `M` maps sensors to kriging points with weight `1/k` for each of a point's `k` merged sensors, and shall set `total_variance = variance + cal_variance`, `total_sd = √total_variance`, and `z_total = error / total_sd`, with `z_total` null when `total_sd = 0` or `cal_variance` is null; `cal_variance` is computed from the rebuilt inputs whether or not `inputs_reproduced` is true.

## Coverage Diagnostics

- [x] **EVAL-UNC-001**: Evaluation shall compute `cal_variance_raw` for every matchup as EVAL-TOTVAR-002 and EVAL-TOTVAR-003 compute `cal_variance` but with each fit's uncorrected covariance, and set `z_total_raw = error / √(variance + cal_variance_raw)`, null when that is 0 or `cal_variance_raw` is null.
- [x] **EVAL-UNC-002**: For each of `z`, `z_total_raw`, and `z_total` over the unflagged matchups counted by its coverage (EVAL-METRIC-002), evaluation shall report the ±1 SD and ±2 SD coverage with their day-bootstrap intervals, the inflation factors `k68 = q(0.683)²` and `k95 = (q(0.954) / 2)²` where `q(p)` is the empirical `p` quantile of its absolute value, each with its day-bootstrap interval, and its mean and standard deviation.
- [x] **EVAL-UNC-003**: Evaluation shall compute, for each kriged hour with at least two kriging-input sensors of `fit_status` `fitted` whose `ref_site_id` has an unflagged observation at the hour, `r_i = pm25_calibrated − obs` over those sensors and `s_H` their mean; report `σ²_shared = max(0, var_H(s_H) − mean_H(var_i(r_i) / n_H))`, the number of hours it is computed over, and the ±1 SD and ±2 SD coverage of `error / √(total_variance + σ²_shared)` over the matchups counted by `cov1_total`; and shall not add `σ²_shared` to `total_variance`.
- [x] **EVAL-UNC-005**: Evaluation shall compute `σ²_shared` as EVAL-UNC-003 does from the hours on odd UTC days of the month only and score with it the unflagged matchups counted by `cov1_total` on even days, and the reverse, and shall write to `coverage_diagnostics.csv` for each direction, as kinds `z_total_shared_odd_on_even` and `z_total_shared_even_on_odd`, the `σ²_shared` used, the number of hours it is computed over, and the ±1 SD and ±2 SD coverage of `error / √(total_variance + σ²_shared)` with day-bootstrap intervals over the scored days; and shall not add either `σ²_shared` to `total_variance`.
- [x] **EVAL-UNC-004**: Evaluation shall write the results of EVAL-UNC-002 and EVAL-UNC-003 to `coverage_diagnostics.csv`, and `summary.md` shall state the calibration-variance formula — per-sensor parameter variance plus residual variance on the diagonal, shared pooled-parameter covariance between pooled sensors of one `as_of`, propagated as `λᵀ M Σ_s Mᵀ λ` with the covariance inflated by `n / n_eff` — and the three coverages with their difference.

## Monte Carlo

- [x] **EVAL-MC-001**: Evaluation shall select `mc_hours` hours with `inputs_reproduced` from the window's kriged hours using a random generator seeded with `seed`, stratified across tertiles of the hour's mean monitor `obs` and preferring hours on distinct days, and shall select every eligible hour when fewer than `mc_hours` are eligible.
- [x] **EVAL-MC-002**: For each selected hour and each of `mc_draws` draws, evaluation shall draw each fitted sensor's `(a, b)` from `N(θ̂_i, C_i)` independently, one `(a, b)` per `fit_as_of` from `N(θ̂_p, C_p)` shared by every pooled sensor using it, and each sensor's residual from `N(0, σ̂²_i)` independently, where `θ̂` are the stored slope and intercept; recalibrate each sensor as `max(0, a + b x + residual)`; merge co-located sensors as fusion does; and krige the monitors' coordinates with a PyKrige `OrdinaryKriging` built with the hour's fixed variogram (EVAL-TOTVAR-001).
- [x] **EVAL-MC-003**: Evaluation shall set each sampled matchup's Monte Carlo `cal_variance` to the variance of its predictions across draws, and shall repeat the propagation with pooled parameters drawn independently per sensor, reporting both beside the analytic `cal_variance`.

## Residual Decomposition

- [x] **EVAL-DECOMP-001**: For each fit with rebuilt pairs, evaluation shall compute the detector-noise term (a) as the mean over the fit's pairs of `(CORRECTION_SLOPE · b)² · mean((A − B)² / 4) / k`, where `A` and `B` are the channel values of the sensor's `k` snapshots in the pair's hour that `aggregate_hourly` aggregates (no QC flags, non-null `pm25_corrected`) and `b` the fit's slope.
- [x] **EVAL-DECOMP-002**: Evaluation shall compute the reference term (b) as `σ_ref²` at each of two hourly precision bounds, 0.5 µg/m³ (Teledyne T640) and 1.8 µg/m³ (Met One BAM-1020), and the remainder (c) as `σ̂² − (a) − (b)`, reporting a negative remainder as 0 and counting the fits for which it was.
- [x] **EVAL-DECOMP-003**: Evaluation shall report the pair-weighted shares of (a), (b), and (c) in `σ̂²` overall, for fitted fits, and for pooled fits, at each reference bound, and shall not add (a) or (b) to any variance used in EVAL-TOTVAR or EVAL-MC.

## Variogram Sensitivity

- [x] **EVAL-SENS-001**: For each kriged hour, evaluation shall fit the candidate variogram model not recorded on the hour's row to the hour's kriging points with fusion's settings (FUS-VAR-001), krige the monitors' coordinates with both the recorded variogram and the alternative, and report the root-mean-square difference of the two predictions, null when the alternative is not eligible (FUS-VAR-003).

## Error Budget

- [x] **EVAL-BUDGET-001**: Evaluation shall write an error budget with columns `source`, `stage`, `type` (`random` or `systematic-shared`), `handling` (`quantified-included`, `quantified-diagnostic`, or `listed-not-modeled`), and `magnitude`, as CSV and as a rendered table figure.
- [x] **EVAL-BUDGET-002**: The error budget shall contain at least the rows: PurpleAir detector noise; Barkjohn correction coefficients; AirNow reference measurement error; AirNow preliminary data, `Value` vs `RawConcentration`, and flatline flags; per-sensor calibration fit parameters; pooled calibration fit parameters; calibration residual (representativeness, siting, model error); regression dilution; kriging interpolation error; variogram fit; and monitors as calibration targets for nearby sensors — each with the stage, type, and handling given in the evaluation design, the last with handling `quantified-diagnostic`.
- [x] **EVAL-BUDGET-003**: Where a budget row's magnitude is quantified by the run (detector noise, calibration parameters, calibration residual, kriging interpolation, variogram sensitivity, AirNow flag rate, slope distribution, and the median `|lomo_error| − |control_error|` of EVAL-LOMO-004 for monitors as calibration targets), evaluation shall fill it from the run with units; otherwise — including when leave one monitor out was skipped or left no unflagged row with both errors — it shall give the published value or "not quantified".

## Figures

- [x] **EVAL-FIG-001**: Evaluation shall write the figures `timeseries`, `scatter`, `coverage`, `bias_vs_pooled`, `decomposition`, `mc_vs_analytic`, `error_budget`, `lomo_rmse`, `baselines_rmse`, `reliability`, `qq_ztotal`, `maps_pm25`, `maps_sd`, and `maps_pm25_10` as PNG at 300 dpi, each beside a CSV of the same name holding the data drawn.
- [x] **EVAL-FIG-002**: Every figure shall be between 3.3 and 6.5 inches wide, label every data axis with its quantity and units, and have every text element inside the saved canvas.
- [x] **EVAL-FIG-003**: The `timeseries` figure shall plot per-hour bias and RMSE of the `unflagged` subset, coloured by UTC date on a sequential colormap with a date colorbar, with the interquartile range of the hour's matchup errors; the `scatter` figure shall plot `surface` against `obs` with the 1:1 line and `r2`; the `coverage` figure shall show ±1 SD and ±2 SD coverage with kriging SD and with total SD against 68.3% and 95.4%; the `bias_vs_pooled` figure shall plot per-hour bias against `pooled_share` with a least-squares line.
- [x] **EVAL-FIG-004**: Evaluation shall choose four hours from the window's kriged hours of variogram class `regular` (from every kriged hour when none is `regular`), on four distinct days, one from each quartile of the hour's mean monitor `obs` — relaxing the distinct-day rule when too few days qualify, and taking every candidate hour when there are fewer than four — and draw them through the maps component as `maps_pm25` (estimate), `maps_sd` (standard deviation), and `maps_pm25_10` (estimate with `pm25_max = 10`), each a small-multiples figure on one fixed scale with sensors and monitors overlaid.
- [x] **EVAL-FIG-005**: Evaluation shall report the window's maximum surface value and maximum SD across kriged hours against the maps' `pm25_max` and `sd_max`, and the fraction of cells above each.
- [x] **EVAL-FIG-006**: The `lomo_rmse` figure shall show, per monitor, the RMSE of `stored_error`, `control_error`, and `lomo_error` over its unflagged rows side by side; the `baselines_rmse` figure shall show the unflagged overall RMSE and bias of each method of `baselines.csv` with their intervals; the `reliability` figure shall plot the bin mean `obs` of EVAL-COND-001 against bin mean `surface` with interval bars, the 1:1 line, and each bin's count; the `qq_ztotal` figure shall plot sorted unflagged `z_total` against standard-normal quantiles with the 1:1 line.
- [x] **EVAL-FIG-007**: Evaluation shall write `figures.md` listing every figure it wrote, numbered in the order of EVAL-FIG-001, each with a one-sentence caption naming what is plotted, the subset, and the units.

## Runs and Report

- [x] **EVAL-RUN-001**: Evaluation shall expose `evaluate(archive_uri, start, end, out_dir, settings) -> EvaluationSummary`, which writes into `out_dir` the files `matchups.csv`, `metrics.csv`, `availability.csv`, `fit_uncertainty.csv`, `montecarlo.csv`, `lomo.csv`, `lomo_by_monitor.csv`, `baselines.csv`, `baseline_paired.csv`, `decomposition.csv`, `variogram_sensitivity.csv`, `variogram.csv`, `coverage_diagnostics.csv`, `anomaly.csv`, `reliability.csv`, `conditional_bias.csv`, `spike_points.csv`, `spikes.csv`, `spike_tiers.csv`, `pooling.csv`, `per_monitor.csv`, `lag.csv`, `error_budget.csv`, the figures of EVAL-FIG-001, `figures.md`, `summary.md`, and `run.json`, and returns the headline metrics.
- [x] **EVAL-RUN-002**: `summary.md` shall state the hours and days available and the gaps; the headline (`unflagged`) metrics and their `all` equivalents; coverage with kriging SD and with total SD; the median ratio of Monte Carlo to analytic `cal_variance`; the decomposition shares; the maps' clipping; the leave-one-monitor-out RMSEs and median difference of EVAL-LOMO-004 with the meaning of its sign; the baseline table of EVAL-BASE-003; and the caveats that AirNow data are preliminary, the matchup count of each split group, that weather differs between days, that monitors are calibration targets for nearby sensors, that the variogram nugget and the calibration residual can partly count the same noise, that the residual term overlaps the spatial variability the kriging variance describes, that variogram uncertainty is in neither term, and that residuals of different sensors in the same hour are treated as independent.
- [x] **EVAL-RUN-003**: `run.json` shall record the window, the evaluation and map settings, the seed, the archive URI, the package version; `git_commit`, `git_dirty` (whether `git status --porcelain` lists anything), and, when dirty, `git_diff_sha256` (the SHA-256 of `git diff HEAD`), each null when the code does not run from a git checkout; `matchups_sha256` (the SHA-256 of the written `matchups.csv`); and, from `partition_objects` over the AirNow observation partitions read, `airnow_objects` (their count), `airnow_last_modified` (the latest `last_modified`, null when none is reported), and `airnow_manifest_sha256` (the SHA-256 over the objects sorted by key of each one's `key`, `etag`, `size`, and `last_modified`, omitting null fields).
- [x] **EVAL-RUN-004**: Two evaluations of the same window with the same settings and seed against an unchanged archive shall write identical CSV files.
- [x] **EVAL-RUN-005**: `summary.md` shall open with a section answering, in this order and each with its number, its interval, and the verdict of the governing rule: where kriging's skill comes from (EVAL-ANOM-004); what the pooled fraction near a monitor does to the bias (EVAL-POOL-003); the inflation factors, how the `n / n_eff` correction and `σ²_shared` change coverage, and the coverage with `σ²_shared` estimated and scored on opposite days (EVAL-UNC-002, EVAL-UNC-003, EVAL-UNC-005); whether kriging beats the `hour_mean`, `idw`, and `rf` baselines (EVAL-BASE-005); how much leave one monitor out worsens the error at the monitors and whether it grows with `n_fitted_to_x` (EVAL-LOMO-006, EVAL-LOMO-007); whether the level-dependent bias survives the `pred_level` split, its lowest-to-highest bias against the `level` split's, and whether the surface is over-smoothed or over-dispersed (EVAL-COND-003); and how much of the affected days' RMSE the spikes account for, by tier (EVAL-SPIKE-004, EVAL-SPIKE-005).

- [x] **EVAL-RUN-006**: The `unflagged` and `all` rows of `summary.md`'s headline accuracy table shall carry the bias and RMSE intervals of the `overall` rows of `metrics.csv` for the same subset.

## Spikes

- [x] **EVAL-SPIKE-001**: For each kriged hour, evaluation shall flag as a spike every kriging point whose value `z` satisfies `z − median > 5 × 1.4826 × MAD` and `z > 20` µg/m³, where `median` and `MAD` are the median and median absolute deviation of the hour's point values, and shall write `spike_points.csv` with `hour`, the point's sensor `site_id`s, `z`, `median`, and `MAD`.
- [x] **EVAL-SPIKE-002**: Evaluation shall label each matchup's hour `affected` in the `spike_hour` split when the hour has a spike point, else `other`, and its day `affected` in the `spike_day` split when its UTC day holds an affected hour, else `other`.
- [x] **EVAL-SPIKE-003**: For each affected hour, evaluation shall predict each matchup monitor's value as EVAL-LOMO-003 predicts (fusion's `select_variogram`, clamped at 0, null below `min_points`) once from all the hour's points (control) and once from the points with the spike points removed, and write `spikes.csv` with one row per (affected hour, monitor) holding `hour`, `site_id`, `obs`, `flagged`, `stored_error`, `control_error`, `without_error`, and `delta = without − control`, without writing to the archive.
- [x] **EVAL-SPIKE-004**: `summary.md` shall report, over unflagged matchups, each affected hour's bias and RMSE from `stored_error` and from `stored_error + delta`; each affected day's RMSE with the affected hours' errors taken both ways; and the headline metrics over all hours and over the hours not affected.

## Common Offset and Within-Hour Anomaly

- [x] **EVAL-ANOM-001**: Over the unflagged matchups of hours with at least 5 unflagged matchups, for each method `kriging` (prediction `surface`), `idw`, `rf`, and `hour_mean`, with `e = prediction − obs`, evaluation shall compute the hour's common offset `ē_H` (mean `e` over the hour's matchups) and anomaly `a = e − ē_H`, and report the offset share `Σ ē_H² / Σ e²` and the anomaly share `Σ a² / Σ e²` over the matchups, each with its day-bootstrap interval.

- [x] **EVAL-ANOM-005**: Over the matchups of EVAL-ANOM-001, evaluation shall split the monitors' variance into the across-hour part `V_across = mean((mean_H(obs) − mean(obs))²)` and the within-hour part `V_within = mean((obs − mean_H(obs))²)`, report each one's share of `V_across + V_within`, and report for each method the across-hour variance explained `1 − mean(ē_H²) / V_across` and the within-hour variance explained `1 − mean(a²) / V_within`, each with its day-bootstrap interval, in `anomaly.csv`.
